Help & frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about submitting and tracking an RMA request.

📦

What is an RMA request?

RMA stands for Return Merchandise Authorisation. It is the official process for returning a faulty or incorrect terminal to MultiSafepay so it can be repaired or replaced.

You can use this portal to register your return request. Once submitted, our support team will review it and send you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.

🔢

What do I need to submit a request?

  • The serial number(s) of the terminal(s) you want to return — always 13 characters, found on the back label or in the device settings
  • A brief description of the problem for each device
  • Your company name, contact person and email address
  • A shipping address where we can send the replacement or repaired terminal

Which terminals can be returned?

You can return any terminal that was supplied by MultiSafepay, including the P3 Air, P3H, P3KH, P3 Mix, P2 Pro, P2 Mini, P2 SE, P2 Lite SE, and P2 SmartPads.

If your terminal model is not recognised by the portal, please contact our support team directly.

What happens after I submit?
1
Request received Your RMA request is received by our support team and a ticket is created in our system.
2
Return label sent (within 1 business day) We will email you a prepaid return label and instructions on how to package and ship the device.
3
Ship the terminal Pack the terminal securely and drop it off at the specified carrier location. Use the label we provided.
4
Assessment & resolution Once we receive the device, we assess it and arrange a repair or replacement. You will be kept informed via email.
Make sure the email address you provide is correct — we will use it to send your return label and any further communication about your request.
